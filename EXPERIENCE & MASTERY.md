# THE JOURNEY

## THE BEGINNING
Every character's journey starts at the beginning, and this time it's no different.  When you create your character there are a number of steps you must follow in order to fully flesh them out. 

1. Concept - What is your concept for your character, just think of a few simple words or phrases that sum up who they are
2. Bio Form - Depending on the campaign setting, there will be different Bio forms to choose from.  Each will offer different bonuses and penalties to your Stats & Effort and also you can choose a Racial Ability that they may offer
3. Stats, Effort & Starting Ability - At this point you will have EXPEREINCE to spend to show what your character is good at. The cost of each is as follows:
STATS - 1 point per increase (max 3 in a single STAT)
EFFORT - 1 point per increase (Max 2 in a SINGLE EFFORT)
ABILITY - 2 points per starting ability

You can only choose from Abilities with the STARTING TAG at this point

## Mastery
Rolling a Natural 20 will still earn you a mastery point.  When you have earned 20 points, you are able to Modify an Ability of your choosing.  Consult with your GM about what you'd like to do, Mastery can only be achieved 3 times in the course of your Character's development.
## The XP Pool

As the group you learn from each other and gain rewards either individually or as a group
During play Experience can be gained from the following ways
- Defeating an Encounter
- Defeating a BOSS
- GM Fiat (Reward or Player doing something cool)
- Rolling a Natural 1 & Keeping it
- End of the session

This Experience will go into the "XP Pool" which players can use during the game in the following ways:

- Succeed at a Dying check
- Re roll any number of dice equal to the XP spent and take the new result
- Gain an item for use (GM Approval)
- Narrative clues or 

When the GM declares that they are at a Safe Haven or its the End of the Session, the XP that remains in the XP Pool is divided amongst players as they see fit.  This XP is now known as Banked Experience.

Banked Experience can be spent in the following manner:

 - Boosting a STAT
 - Boosting an EFFORT
 - Gain a HEART
 - Gain an Ability with the MILESTONE TAG
 - Train an Existing ABILITY with the PROGRESSIVE TAG
 - Learn a Language

When spending you declare how much XP you wish to spend, then roll that many relevant EFFORT dice ( GM tells you which EFFORT you can roll).  You then gain Training Points equal to the STAT+EFFORT rolled.  Once you've acquired enough training points equal to the amount of HEARTS the Boost is worth, you gain that Ability, the next stage of the Progressive Milestone, +1 to relevant STAT, EFFORT or HEART.

*Idea around tracking how much XP players have earnt, this will tell the GM how tough to build the encounters. Also at certain points players gain a HEART *
